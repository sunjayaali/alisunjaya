If you're not a cyclist, these numbers might look a little confusing. Here's what they actually mean.

### Power — How Hard You're Pedaling

**Power** is measured in watts (W). Think of it as how much effort you're putting into the pedals. The harder you push, the higher the number.

### Heart Rate — How Hard Your Body Is Working

**Heart rate** is measured in beats per minute (bpm). It tells you how hard your body is working during the ride.

### Cadence — How Fast Your Legs Are Spinning

**Cadence** is measured in revolutions per minute (rpm). It tells you how many times the pedals complete a full rotation every minute.

## Rest Paves the Long Road Ahead

The title comes from [**18×2 Beyond Youthful Days**](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt31039829/), a movie that stayed with me. I liked the idea of using the line for this ride because it fits the mood of the day. It's a reminder that sometimes you need to slow down, take a break, and enjoy where you are before continuing on the road ahead. So, for this ride, it felt like the perfect title.
